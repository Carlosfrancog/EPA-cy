# MAPA Evidence Gates v0.1

The Evaluation dashboard adds a transparent, **read-only** scientific evidence verdict to each best observed algorithm run. It checks the provenance (research report schema/manifest SHA/seed), at least three grouped folds, missing class support in folds, and an independent temporal-cohort result.

All existing reports remain valid. Historical runs without research evidence are labelled `insufficient_evidence`. Eligible research studies remain `research_only` with `deployable: false`: all present inputs are synthetic, and no external validation of educational outcomes has been completed.

No statistical significance, population confidence bounds, production qualification or model promotion is inferred. These are prerequisite completeness checks, not a threshold claiming predictive efficacy.
